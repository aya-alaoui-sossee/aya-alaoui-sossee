### 🔬 Research Focus: Translational Oncology & Trustworthy Computational Pathology

> *"The endpoint is not a better model. It is a more trustworthy biological measurement."*  
> — Presented at the **University of Cambridge CCAIM Machine Learning for Healthcare Summer School (2026)** *(Supported by GSK)*

- **Trustworthy TIL Biomarkers in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC):** Designing a three-stage validation strategy (**Establish → Stress-Test → Generalize**) for pathology foundation models and hybrid feature-extraction pipelines quantifying tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) from gigapixel H&E whole-slide images:
  1. **Establish Measurement Validity:** Triangulating AI-derived TIL scores against **CD8 Immunohistochemistry (IHC)** and **expert pathologist assessment** on public cohorts (TCGA-BRCA).
  2. **Stress-Test Across Populations:** Evaluating consistency across **Caucasian-American and African-American** TNBC cohorts while adjusting for clinical (age, tumour stage/grade, treatment) and technical (tissue processing, H&E staining, scanner type) confounders.
  3. **Generalize to New Data-Generating Environments:** External validation on **Moroccan TNBC cohorts** to distinguish true biological signal from dataset- and acquisition-specific noise.
- **Molecular Foundation Models from Routine Histopathology:** Engineered end-to-end Multiple-Instance Learning (MIL) and pathology foundation-model pipelines for actionable mutation prediction (including **EGFR mutation prediction in lung adenocarcinoma**), outperforming baseline benchmarks by **+15%** and reaching **94% benchmark accuracy**.
