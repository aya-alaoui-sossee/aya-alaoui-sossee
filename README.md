### 🔬 Research Interests

- **Trustworthy AI Biomarkers in Oncology:** Quantifying tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) from H&E whole-slide images in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC), with a focus on biomarker reliability and cross-population generalization across underrepresented African and Moroccan cohorts.
- **Molecular Prediction from Histopathology:** Weakly supervised Multiple-Instance Learning (MIL) and pathology foundation models for mutation prediction (including *EGFR* in lung adenocarcinoma) directly from routine whole-slide images.
